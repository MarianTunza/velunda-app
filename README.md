# Velunda

The privacy policy and support pages for **Velunda**, a private journal for
iPhone and iPad.

- Privacy Policy: https://mariantunza.github.io/velunda-app/privacy/
- Support: https://mariantunza.github.io/velunda-app/support/

Velunda collects nothing: your journal stays on your devices and in your own
end-to-end encrypted iCloud.

Contact: contact@getvelunda.com
